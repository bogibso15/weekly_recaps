PLAC+ Week 5 Recap

Tecumseh 49, Perry Central 0

The Perry Central Commodores suffered a 49-0 defeat at home against a physical Tecumseh squad in a non-conference matchup. The Braves' front line controlled the line of scrimmage from the opening drive, building an early multi-possession lead that the Commodores were unable to break down. Perry Central's defensive unit struggled to defend off-tackle runs, allowing several chunk plays on the ground before halftime.

With the loss, Perry Central falls to 1-4 on the year. The Commodores' offensive unit struggled to establish any rhythm, hampered by execution errors and containment failures against an aggressive blitzing scheme that registered three sacks. Quarterback Cruz Yeager was forced into hurried throws under pressure, and the run game could not generate regular positive yardage to move the chains.

Perry Central will look to regroup on the road next Friday night when they travel to face Clarksville.

West Washington 20, Paoli 14

In a crucial Patoka Lake Athletic Conference division clash, West Washington defended its home turf with a tight 20-14 victory over rival Paoli. The Senators' defensive front delivered the defining stand of the game, stopping a late fourth-quarter drive inside the 10-yard line to secure the victory. The win pushes West Washington to 3-2 overall, while Paoli drops to 2-2.

The game was a low-scoring defensive battle throughout the first half, with both team fronts shutting down interior run play. The Senators broke the stalemate in the third quarter with two sequential scoring drives to pull ahead. Paoli mounted a counter-offensive behind their option run game but fell short on their final possession against the Senators' boundary containment.

West Washington will host undefeated Class 1A powerhouse North Daviess next Friday night, while Paoli travels to host Springs Valley.

Mitchell 41, Crawford County 0

The Mitchell Bluejackets secured their first victory of the season, shutting out conference foe Crawford County 41-0 on the road. The Bluejackets' offensive front set a physical tone from the opening kickoff, paving the way for a running game that exceeded 300 total yards. Mitchell built a comfortable cushion before halftime, effectively taking Crawford County out of its baseline ball-control offensive script.

The loss extends Crawford County's winless streak to 0-5 on the year. The Wolfpack struggled to find throwing windows against Mitchell's primary secondary shell, which recorded two interceptions. Crawford County's offense was held to under 100 total yards and surrendered two costly fumbles in their own territory.

Crawford County remains home next week to host Springs Valley, while Mitchell prepares for a PLAC road challenge.

Springs Valley 57, Jennings County 7

Class 1A No. 10 Springs Valley logged its fourth consecutive blowout win, easily handling the 4A Jennings County Panthers 57-7. The Blackhawks' high-powered offense operated at peak efficiency, scoring on five of their first six possessions to cruise to a 4-1 record on the season. Springs Valley's line controlled the line of scrimmage completely, allowing the skill positions to execute at will.

Jennings County had no answers for the Blackhawks' speed on the edges and fell to 2-3. Springs Valley's defensive unit set a dominant tone, recording three sacks and returning a fumble for a touchdown early in the second half. The Blackhawks have outscored their last three opponents by more than 34 points apiece.

Springs Valley hits the road next Friday night at 7:00 p.m. for a highly anticipated PLAC matchup against Crawford County.

Tell City 35, Hancock County (KY) 14

Tell City stepped out of Pocket Athletic Conference action and crossed state lines to secure a 35-14 non-conference road victory over Hancock County (KY). The Marksmen logic returned to form on the ground following their first loss of the season, establishing control of the trenches early in the contest. Tell City's frontline blocking created consistent pathways against the Hornets' interior front.

The win moves the Marksmen to a 4-1 overall record. Brandon Casebolt continued his prominent role in the rushing scheme, recording multiple long carries to help distance the Marksmen on the scoreboard. Defensively, Tell City limited the Hornets' perimeter options and forced two turnovers on downs to stifle third-quarter drives.

North Daviess 54, Cloverdale 7

Class 1A North Daviess maintained its perfect record, routing Cloverdale 54-7 in Elnora to capture their fifth consecutive victory of the season. The Cougars' multi-faceted offensive scheme executed cleanly from the first series, dismantling the Clovers' secondary with explosive downfield plays. North Daviess established a four-touchdown cushion by the second quarter, enabling the coaching staff to mix rotations across the depth chart.

Quarterback Jason Jones paced the offensive attack with a highly efficient night through the air and on the ground, accounting for three total touchdowns. The Cougars' starting offensive line did not yield a sack and cleared open lanes for a collective rushing output exceeding 250 yards. Defensively, North Daviess limited Cloverdale to single-digit rushing yards and forced two second-half interceptions.

North Daviess puts its 5-0 record on the line next Friday night when they hit the road for a non-league challenge against West Washington.

Players of the Week
Offensive Player of the Week: Brandon Casebolt (Tell City)

Casebolt put on an absolute showcase under the Friday night lights, fueling the Marksmen attack to cross state lines and claim a 35-14 road victory over Hancock County (KY). The lightning-fast senior dynamo was untouchable on the perimeter, breaking loose for multiple explosive runs and punishing the Hornets' frontline defense for significant chunk yardage. Casebolt's terrific production unlocked Tell City's game script and demonstrated his ability to beo ne of the most explosive premier backfield weapons in Southern Indiana.

Defensive Player of the Week: Christian Hamilton (Springs Valley)

Hamilton was an absolute terror for opposing playmakers on Friday night, anchoring a ferocious Blackhawks defense that completely dismantled Jennings County in a 57-7 statement victory. Flying across the secondary, Hamilton displayed tremendous closing speed. The senior defensive back was able to fundamentally lock down his half of the field, logging a clutch third-down interception and hitting the ball-carrier to force a spectacular fumble recovery touchdown that broke the Panthers' spirit and protected the team's home momentum.
